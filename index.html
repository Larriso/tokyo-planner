Book these first

Ghibli Museum ticketsNovember tickets went on sale on 10 Oct on the official Lawson Ticket site (lawson.co.jp/ghibli_museum/english.html), ¥1,000 each. Buy for everyone going at once. The museum is closed on Tuesdays.
Karting needs a 1949 Geneva driving permit for each driverJapan only accepts the 1949 Geneva booklet; Lithuania issues it through Regitra. Bring it with your licence and passport.
Timed ticketsBook Shibuya Sky (a sunset slot is around 16:00), teamLab, the tea ceremony, the Fuji tour (with free cancellation) and the Harry Potter tour ahead. Kabuki single-act tickets are sold on the day only.
Tax-free changes on 1 NovUntil 31 Oct shops take the 10% tax off at the till with your passport. From 1 Nov you pay it and claim it back at the airport, so do the big shopping first.
